# Task 5: Random Balanced Stream Evaluation (Unstratified) Report

## 1. Executive Summary & Critical Evaluation
In the manuscript Table 6, the multi-state evaluation relies on a **stratified benchmark** structured with 180 pure-benign, 240 mixed (binomial 50%), and 180 pure-attack batches. To test whether this artificial batch purity artificially inflates performance, a **completely unstratified random balanced stream** of 60,000 flows (50.2% attack) was generated by randomly shuffling all flows prior to batching.

### Critical Comparison to Table 6:
- **Block FPR**: Under random unstratified mixing, P2-tier achieves **Block FPR of 0.241%** (Wilson 95% CI [0.191%, 0.303%]), completely breaking the claimed '0.00% Block FPR' of Table 6!
- **Quarantine FPR**: 75.184% (Wilson 95% CI [74.691%, 75.671%]).
- **Disruptive FPR**: Total disruptive FPR is 75.42%.
- **Attack Density Monotonicity**: True.

## 4. Manuscript Correction Guidance
The manuscript must explicitly state that the 0.00% Block FPR of P2-tier is specific to the stratified benchmark containing pure benign episodes. Under continuously mixed, unstratified traffic, batch-level escalation causes benign flows embedded within attack-bearing batches to suffer collateral blocking.
