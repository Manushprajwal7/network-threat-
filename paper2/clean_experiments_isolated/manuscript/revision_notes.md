## 2. Summary of Policy Optimality & Trade-offs

The audit proves that no static level-to-action policy is Pareto-dominant across all network conditions:
1. **Conservative Policy $P_1$**: Guarantees zero benign Block assignments on both streams (0.00% Block FPR), but incurs 29.90% Quarantine FPR on balanced traffic and 100.00% Quarantine FPR on attack-heavy traffic, sacrificing 1,706,400 OCU in threat containment value relative to $P_{2\text{-tier}}$.
2. **Aggressive Policy $P_{2\text{-tier}}$**: Maximizes threat containment (+1,909,843 OCU combined, a 2.43x gain over $P_1$) and preserves 0.00% Block FPR on balanced traffic, but incurs 100.00% Block FPR on embedded benign flows during massive unsegmented volumetric floods.
3. **Dynamic Switcher (Experiment E9 / Section 4.4)**: Evaluated online dynamic switching ($\bar{T}_b \le 40.0 \land \bar{s}_b \ge 0.50$); matches static $P_{2\text{-tier}}$ combined benefit (+1,909,733 OCU) with 0.00% Block FPR on balanced traffic, demonstrating that well-calibrated static policies achieve optimal performance without runtime switching overhead.
